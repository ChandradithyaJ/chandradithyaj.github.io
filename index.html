Chandradithya Jonnalagadda

Chandra (pronunciation: chun-dra)

I'm a Masters student in Computer Science at Brown University working with George Konidaris and Chen Sun. My research goal is to build intelligent robots that can reliably perform a wide range of complex tasks and safely adapt to unfamiliar tasks and environments. To this end, I am interested in Offline RL, Offline-to-Online RL and Safe RL.

Previously, I graduated with a Bachelors in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Tirupati, where I worked with Ajin George Joseph on Risk-Sensitive RL. During this time, I was fortunate to spend a year as a Student Researcher at Robert Bosch Center for Data Science and AI working with Balaraman Ravindran on Explainable AI for Graphs to ground LLM reasoning.

I'm actively looking for and applying to PhD positions starting Fall 2027!

Outside of research, I enjoy playing sports (mainly cricket and fútbol, although I'd never say no to any sport!), trekking, cooking and editing videos!

Research

Hierarchical RL with Temporally-extended Latent Action World Models

Dan Haramati, Chandradithya S Jonnalagadda, Akhil Bagaria, George Konidaris

RLC 2026 · Model-Based RL in the Era of Generative World Models Workshop · Best Paper Award 🏆

paper

We introduce an offline goal-conditioned framework that learns a high-level world model used to plan subgoals for a value-based model-free RL policy. We propose Temporally-extended Latent Action World Models (Te-LAWMs) as high-level world models, trained via a latent action modeling objective.

Latent Actions from Factorized Transition Effects under Agent Ambiguity

Heejeong Nam, Chandradithya S Jonnalagadda, Harshit Aggarwal, Eric Xu, Randall Balestriero

ICML 2026 · Compositional Learning Workshop

paper / project page / code

We propose Observed Transtion Factorization LAM (OTF-LAM), a latent action mdoel that learns environment-agnostic motion primitives from visual changes and composes them into state-aware latent actions that capture coordinated and independent movements. These latent actions are mapped to real actions using a small amount of action-labeled data.

The Price of Paranoia: Robust Risk-Sensitive Cooperation in Non-Stationary Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

Deep Ganguly, Chandradithya S Jonnalagadda, Pratham Chintamani, Adithya Ananth

AAMAS 2026 · Adaptive Learning Agents Workshop

paper

We propose a MARL algorithm that sustains cooperation between agents in mixed-motive games and social dilemmas by improving robustness to the policy gradient update variance induced by the other agents' uncertainties.
